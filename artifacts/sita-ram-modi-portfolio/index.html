01 / About the researcher

Materials mechanics,
read closely.

Sita Ram Modi is a Postdoctoral Research Associate at IIT Mandi working across experimental and computational materials mechanics. His research profile brings together fracture, fatigue, failure mechanisms, Ti-6Al-4V ELI / TC4 ELI alloys, experimental testing, SEM/EDS, electrochemical characterization, FEM/XFEM, and phase-field approaches.

02 / Research areas

Questions at the
edge of failure.

The work sits between careful experiments and models that make material behaviour legible.

01Fracture mechanics

Experimental and computational study of how cracks initiate, propagate, and shape structural life.

02Fatigue & failure

Fatigue and hold-time effects, failure mechanisms, and life estimation in demanding materials systems.

03Ti-6Al-4V ELI alloys

Materials mechanics research involving titanium alloys including Ti-6Al-4V ELI and TC4 ELI.

04Multiphysics fracture

Fracture analysis where mechanical response is considered alongside coupled physical fields.

05Phase-field & XFEM

Continuum and extended finite-element approaches for representing crack evolution computationally.

06Environmentally assisted fracture

Testing and characterization of fracture response in the presence of an environment.

Current research

A crack is a story,
not just a line.

Current postdoctoral work (2025–present) at the School of Mechanical and Materials Engineering, IIT Mandi, is part of an AR&DB / DRDO project: development of phase-field modelling and machine-learning-based approaches for fracture analysis of composite structures under multiphysics loadings.
